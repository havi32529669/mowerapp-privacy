# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**适用产品**：割草机APP（MowerApp）
**最后更新**：2025-02-13

## 中文版

### 一、概述

济南科亚电子科技有限公司（以下称"我们"）开发的割草机APP（以下称"本应用"）是一款用于控制和管理割草机器人硬件设备的工具类应用。本应用需配合专用割草机器人硬件通过局域网使用。

我们高度重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用会处理哪些信息、如何处理，以及您拥有的权利。

### 二、我们不收集的信息

**本应用不设置用户账号，不要求注册或登录，不会将您的个人信息上传至我们的服务器。**

我们不收集、不存储、不共享您的姓名、手机号、邮箱、身份证件等身份信息，也不会为广告或追踪目的收集任何数据。本应用不集成第三方广告 SDK、不集成第三方数据分析 SDK。

### 三、本应用在您设备上处理的信息

以下信息仅存储在您的设备本地，不会传输至我们或任何第三方：

| 信息类型 | 用途 | 存储位置 |
|---|---|---|
| 作业地图、边界与路径数据 | 生成割草路径、执行导航作业 | 设备本地数据库 |
| 设备连接配置（机器人 IP、端口、设备编号） | 连接您的割草机器人 | 设备本地配置文件 |
| 作业历史记录（时长、覆盖面积等） | 供您查看历史作业情况 | 设备本地数据库 |
| 应用偏好设置（语言、显示选项等） | 保持您的使用习惯 | 设备本地配置文件 |
| 离线地图瓦片缓存 | 无网络环境下显示地图 | 设备本地缓存 |

卸载本应用即会删除上述全部本地数据。您也可在应用内删除单个地图、路径或作业记录。

### 四、系统权限说明

**本地网络（Local Network）**
您的割草机器人在其自建 Wi-Fi 热点下以固定局域网地址（10.0.5.1）提供服务。本应用需要本地网络权限才能与该设备通信，用于下发控制指令、读取设备状态和同步地图数据。此权限仅用于访问您自己的硬件设备，不用于扫描或探测您家庭网络中的其他设备。

**位置信息（使用期间）**
自 iOS 13 起，读取当前所连 Wi-Fi 网络名称（SSID）需要定位授权。本应用请求此权限的唯一目的是确认您已连接到正确的割草机热点，并在连接向导中给出相应提示。**您的位置坐标不会被记录、不会被存储、不会离开您的设备。** 拒绝此权限不影响其他功能，仅会导致应用无法自动识别当前 Wi-Fi 名称。

**文件访问**
仅在您主动使用文件选择器导入或导出 CSV 格式的路径数据时触发，我们不会在后台访问您的文件。

### 五、第三方地图服务

当您使用在线地图功能时，本应用会直接向您所选择的地图服务商请求地图瓦片。这些请求由您的设备直接发往该服务商，我们不参与、不代理、不留存。

可能涉及的服务商包括（取决于您在设置中的选择）：高德地图、必应地图。

在此过程中，相关服务商可能获知您的 IP 地址以及您所浏览的地图区域。这些行为受各服务商自身的隐私政策约束，建议您查阅相应条款。若您仅使用离线地图，则不会产生此类请求。

### 六、数据传输与安全

本应用与割草机器人之间的通信发生在设备自建的局域网内，使用明文 TCP/HTTP 协议。这是由硬件设备的通信接口决定的。该通信不经过互联网，仅限于您与机器人所处的同一局域网范围内。

我们建议您为割草机器人的 Wi-Fi 热点设置密码，以防止未授权设备接入。

### 七、儿童隐私

本应用为割草机器人配套控制工具，面向成年设备持有者，不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

### 八、您的权利

由于本应用不收集您的个人信息，也不在服务器端保存任何与您相关的数据，因此无需向我们提出数据查询、更正或删除请求。您对本应用产生的全部数据拥有完全控制权：可在应用内删除，或通过卸载应用一次性清除。

### 九、政策变更

如本应用未来新增涉及数据收集的功能，我们将更新本政策并在应用内或本页面显著位置告知。请留意页首的"最后更新"日期。

## English Version

### 1. Overview

MowerApp (the "App"), developed by Jinan Keya Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"), is a utility application for controlling and managing robotic lawn mower hardware. The App is designed to be used together with dedicated robotic mower hardware over a local area network.

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the App handles, how it is handled, and what rights you have.

### 2. Information We Do Not Collect

**The App has no user accounts. It does not require registration or sign-in, and it does not upload your personal information to our servers.**

We do not collect, store, or share your name, phone number, email address, or any identity documents. We do not collect any data for advertising or tracking purposes. The App contains no third-party advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs.

### 3. Information Processed on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

| Type | Purpose | Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Work maps, boundaries, and path data | Generating mowing paths and running navigation tasks | Local database on device |
| Device connection settings (robot IP, port, device ID) | Connecting to your mower | Local configuration file |
| Task history (duration, area covered) | Letting you review past work | Local database on device |
| App preferences (language, display options) | Preserving your settings | Local configuration file |
| Offline map tile cache | Displaying maps without an internet connection | Local cache on device |

Uninstalling the App deletes all of this local data. You may also delete individual maps, paths, or task records from within the App.

### 4. Permissions

**Local Network**
Your robotic mower provides its services at a fixed local address (10.0.5.1) on the Wi-Fi hotspot it creates. The App requires Local Network access to communicate with that device in order to send control commands, read device status, and synchronize map data. This permission is used solely to reach your own hardware. The App does not scan for or probe other devices on your home network.

**Location (When In Use)**
Since iOS 13, reading the name (SSID) of the currently connected Wi-Fi network requires location authorization. The App requests this permission for the sole purpose of confirming that you are connected to the correct mower hotspot and guiding you through the connection process. **Your location coordinates are never recorded, never stored, and never leave your device.** Denying this permission does not affect other features; it only prevents the App from automatically identifying the current Wi-Fi network name.

**File Access**
Triggered only when you actively use the file picker to import or export path data in CSV format. We do not access your files in the background.

### 5. Third-Party Map Services

When you use online map features, the App requests map tiles directly from the map provider you have selected. These requests go from your device to that provider. We neither proxy nor retain them.

Depending on your choice in settings, providers may include AutoNavi (Amap) and Bing Maps.

In the course of these requests, the relevant provider may learn your IP address and the map area you are viewing. This is governed by that provider's own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review. If you use offline maps only, no such requests are made.

### 6. Data Transmission and Security

Communication between the App and the robotic mower takes place on the local network created by the hardware itself, using plain TCP/HTTP. This is determined by the communication interface of the hardware. This traffic does not traverse the internet and is confined to the local network shared by you and the mower.

We recommend setting a password on your mower's Wi-Fi hotspot to prevent unauthorized devices from connecting.

### 7. Children's Privacy

The App is a companion controller for robotic mower hardware, intended for adult device owners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

### 8. Your Rights

Because the App does not collect your personal information and we hold no server-side data about you, there is no need to submit access, correction, or deletion requests to us. You retain full control over all data the App creates: delete it within the App, or remove all of it at once by uninstalling the App.

### 9. Changes to This Policy

If future versions of the App introduce features that involve data collection, we will update this policy and provide notice within the App or prominently on this page. Please check the "Last updated" date at the top.
